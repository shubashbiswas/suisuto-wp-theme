Terms of Service

The protocols, client commitments, and craftsmanship standards governing commissions, orders, and services with the House of Sui Suto.

SECTION 01

Atelier Commissions & Orders

Every Sui Suto creation is hand-loomed and individually finished. By placing an order or bespoke inquiry through our storefront or client concierge, you agree that your purchase is intended exclusively for personal adornment and private collection, not for unauthorized commercial resale or modification.

We reserve the right to decline or cancel commissions where production capacity at regional artisan clusters is constrained by climate, harvest cycles, or seasonal humidity.

SECTION 02

Material Integrity & Human Craftsmanship

Our textiles—including pure Dhaka Jamdani, Baluchari silk, Mulberry silk, and plant-fermented indigo—are woven entirely on traditional wooden frame and pit looms without automated intervention.

Subtle irregularities in yarn thickness, slubs, micro-variations in weft density, and natural botanical dye nuances are authentic hallmarks of genuine human craftsmanship and are not classified as manufacturing defects.

SECTION 03

Global White-Glove Dispatch & Duties

All international shipments are dispatched via tracked, insured express couriers in bespoke archival presentation boxes. Delivery timeframes reflect individual garment preparation and riverine climate conditioning.

For shipments outside Bangladesh and India, import duties, customs tariffs, and local taxes may be levied upon arrival in accordance with destination country laws and are the sole responsibility of the client unless explicitly covered by a concierge agreement.

SECTION 04

Returns, Exchanges & Archival Care

Due to the delicate nature of heirloom handlooms, returns are accepted within 14 calendar days of confirmed delivery provided the garment is in its original, unworn state with all atelier security seals, archival muslin wrappers, and tags intact.

Custom-tailored, made-to-measure bridal commissions and cut textile lengths are strictly non-returnable once work on the loom has commenced.

SECTION 05

Intellectual Property & Artisan Heritage

All motifs, architectural cuts, photography, narrative texts, and brand assets displayed on Sui Suto remain the exclusive intellectual property of the House of Sui Suto and its partnering Bengal artisan cooperatives. Unauthorized reproduction or commercial emulation is strictly prohibited.
