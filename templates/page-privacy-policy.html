Privacy Policy

How the House of Sui Suto safeguards client discretion, personal measurements, transactional records, and private communications.

SECTION 01

Client Discretion & Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information required to fulfill orders, conduct bespoke tailor fittings, and deliver white-glove courier services. This includes your name, contact coordinates, delivery destination, tailored measurements, and purchase history.

We treat all client consultations with monastic confidentiality. We never sell, lease, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

SECTION 02

Secure Payment & Encryption Standards

Payment card data is encrypted using 256-bit SSL protocols and processed directly through certified PCI-DSS compliant financial gateways. Sui Suto never stores raw credit card details or bank account credentials on our servers.

SECTION 03

Cookies & Regional Storefront Adaptation

We utilize minimal, privacy-focused session cookies solely to preserve your active shopping bag, maintain regional currency preferences across our multisite storefronts (USD, BDT, INR), and remember account authentication.

We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking scripts or advertising pixels.

SECTION 04

Courier & Artisan Attribution Sharing

To fulfill physical delivery, your shipping address and contact number are shared strictly with our contracted insured global courier partners (e.g. DHL Express, FedEx). Atelier weaving clusters only receive sizing dimensions and order numbers without client identity markers.

SECTION 05

Your Rights & Concierge Contact

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR and regional data standards), you retain the right to request a full transcript of your stored personal data or request permanent deletion of your client profile at any time.

For privacy inquiries or data removal, please contact our confidential data officer at privacy@suisuto.com.
